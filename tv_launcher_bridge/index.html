TV Launcher Bridge

The bridge runs inside Home Assistant OS. No PC or TV app is needed.

On the TV, open Settings → System → Developer options → Wireless debugging. Enable it, then choose “Pair device with pairing code”.

The connection port differs from the pairing port. Discover can find it before opening the pairing dialog.
